Daughter of Bones

Welcome to the current project in progress! Daughter of Bones is the very first game that Drowning In Dreams Studios is making.

Project overview

It is a 2D survival roguelike with upgrade trees, randomly generated maps and different weapons to match your playstyle. The prototype will include one character, one basic map, two or three enemies with various attack patterns, two weapons, a main menu, UI and an upgrade tree.
