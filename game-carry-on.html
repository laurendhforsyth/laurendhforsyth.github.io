Carry On

Carry On explores the five stages of grief through a narrative game. Each world represents a different emotional stage and uses its environment, mechanics and atmosphere to communicate that feeling.

Project overview

World 1 — Denial: enemies move towards you when you look away, showing that grief needs to be faced. World 2 — Anger: the player uses emotions as a weapon. World 3 — Bargaining: “what if” statements grant powers. World 4 — Depression: a quiet fog and a fairy guide explore isolation and accepting help. World 5 — Acceptance: the player returns to full colour and begins to move through grief.
