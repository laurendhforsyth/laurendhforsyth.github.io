Project overview

During the time I worked for Games by Stitch (also known as Stitch Media), I had the opportunity to work on their website. Their website is hosted using a CMS service, and I created and updated pages, and updated their navigation bar.

Navigation Bar

I updated their navigation bar to add a hover effect, highlight the contact button, and added in social media links to their respective websites. I also added hover effects to the dropdown menus, and submenus for each, if needed.

Game Page Updates

Games by Stitch has several games which I also updated the webpages for. I updated the pages for Broken Spectre, Flow Weaver, Terrorarium and Rival Books of Aster, adding in call to action buttons, as well as updating the descriptions and icons for each. If the game had subpages, I also updated those with additional call to action buttons (such as the comic “The Blacksaw” for Broken Spectre, and press kits for each) and descriptions for those as well.
